Palash Kumar Sarker is a Senior Scientific Officer at the National Institute of Biotechnology (NIB) in Bangladesh, specializing in the field of Microbial Biotechnology. With over 645 citations and an h-index of 8, his research primarily focuses on the identification and characterization of microbial enzymes, waste-to-energy technologies, and molecular diagnostics. His most influential work includes the optimization of extracellular amylase and alkaline protease production from various Bacillus species, as well as significant studies on the prevalence and antibiotic susceptibility of clinical pathogens.
Additionally, Mr. Sarker has explored sustainable energy solutions through Microbial Fuel Cells (MFCs) and investigated genetic polymorphisms related to diseases like Type 2 Diabetes within the Bangladeshi population. His diverse body of work contributes to both industrial biotechnology and public health, reflecting a commitment to advancing biological sciences in South Asia.

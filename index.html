About Us

Microorganisms like bacteria, yeast, virus, fungi, etc. have economic importance and industrial values. To have maximum benefit from these microbes, presently the Division has emphasized research for the delivery of eco-friendly products and processes in cost-effective manner. Our research interests span from the industrial enzymes to the production of any microbial products from wild or genetically modified microbial species. We are dedicated to researching various microbes to create innovative probiotics for human well-being and enzymes for industrial applications. Join us in our journey to explore the potential of microbiology for a healthier future.

Our Research

The MBD Lab focuses on advancing microbial biotechnology with applications in human health and sustainable energy. Our research encompasses the development of human probiotics, the discovery and engineering of novel drugs derived from microbial sources, and the characterization of microbial enzymes for industrial and therapeutic use. We also explore microbial fuel cells as a promising platform for bioenergy generation. Through these interconnected efforts, the lab aims to harness the vast potential of microorganisms to address challenges in medicine, biotechnology, and renewable energy.

Our Projects

Our current research project focuses on the development of human probiotics and the discovery of novel drugs from microbial sources. We are actively isolating and characterizing beneficial microbial strains to create effective probiotic formulations that support gut health and overall human well-being. In parallel, we explore microbial metabolites and bioactive compounds as potential candidates for new therapeutic agents. Through these efforts, our work aims to bridge microbial science with practical applications in healthcare and drug development.

Our Team

Our research team brings together scientists, research fellows, thesis students, and technical staff with diverse expertise and complementary roles. The team currently includes one Principal Scientific Officer (PSO), one Senior Scientific Officer (SSO), and one Scientific Officer (SO) serving as staff scientists, supported by research fellows, thesis students, a Junior Experimental Officer, a Scientific Assistant, and a Laboratory Assistant.
